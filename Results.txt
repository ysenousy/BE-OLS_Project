Results
=======

Step 1: TTL Metadata Extraction
-------------------------------

119 ontology TTL files were processed.

117/119 parsed successfully (98.3% success rate).

2 files failed to parse because of source-file issues:
- sao.ttl: unbound xs: prefix in the source TTL
- th-building.ttl: maximum recursion depth exceeded while parsing

Metadata quality:
- Mean completeness: 65.6% across all ontologies
- 71 ontologies include license metadata
- 86 ontologies include version metadata
- 72 ontologies include creator metadata

Ontology structure averages:
- Mean classes per ontology: 120
- Mean properties per ontology: 62
- Mean axioms/triples per ontology: 1,982
- Mean annotation coverage: 69.8%
- Mean comment coverage: 60.0%


Step 2: Academic Paper Search
-----------------------------

Current Step 2 uses OpenAlex search with ontology-specific profiles from
ontology_search_profiles.json. Semantic Scholar and OpenAIRE are not used in
the current pipeline.

The final paper output was cleaned using profile-level filters for ambiguous
ontology names and generic domains. The output keeps papers classified as:
- exact_ontology
- ontology_family
- reuse_application

Paper search output:
- Ontologies searched: 119
- Total paper results kept: 265
- Ontologies with >=1 paper result: 68
- Ontologies with 0 paper results: 51
- Mean results per ontology with results: 3.9
- Mean results per ontology overall: 2.2

Match type distribution:
- exact_ontology: 69
- ontology_family: 46
- reuse_application: 150

Paper metrics:
- Mean title/query overlap: 0.1286
- Mean abstract/query overlap: 0.0257
- Mean citation count: 38.7
- Median paper year: 2022
- Ontologies with direct DOI hit: 0/119
